# draft-part changelog

| Version | Date | Change | Golden-set result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1.0 | 17 Nov 2026 | First version (L-DRAFT.parts, BLD-C072): replaces `canvas-assist` for the live draft. One agent asked once per part (title and summary, how often, who it affects, what it's worth, area), the parts in parallel. Every answer quotes the person's own words, checked by the gateway with the DEC-095 rules from `grounding.py` (re-ask once, then drop); an English copy of a voice note or another language is context only (DEC-108) | Fake-mode harness passes (no-stack test); real-route numbers in UAT |
| 0.1.1 | 17 Nov 2026 | Examples only: the who-it-affects list uses L-SORT's labels ("AMC clients", "Issuer and corporate clients"); when `rules/sorting.md` is present its lists are the ones offered, with the cues from `rules/drafting.md` | Fake-mode harness passes |
| 0.1.2 | 17 Nov 2026 | Gates and checks only, the prompt's instructions unchanged (BLD-C033, BLD-C035): the golden set now gates the model before the gateway mends it, at most 1 of 12 answers dropped and at most 2 of 12 refused on the first ask (3 in the first cut of 0.1.2, unreleased); the summary's sentence count no longer ends a sentence at "Rs." or "e.g."; the fake clips its summary to 600 characters at a word and picks at most 8 groups | Fake-mode harness passes (no-stack test); the new gates are enforced on a real route (UAT) |
| 0.2.0 | 20 Nov 2026 | Business ideas (DEC-118 (4), BLD-C201, BLD-C202, BLD-C205): the title part also says what kind it is (`kind`: `business_idea`, `problem` or null) with one quote of the person's own words (`kind_evidence`), held by the same grounding checks as every other quote (word for word from one message's own text, never the English copy); after the one re-ask a kind still unbacked is dropped on its own and the title and summary are kept. For a new business idea the summary describes the new offer: what it is, who it is for and what it would do for them, only as the person said it; the title names the offer. Something new KFintech could offer goes under New business when it is offered. New example `07-business-title.json` (in the prompt). The golden set grows from 12 to 20 cases (PRIM, loan against mutual funds, short, long, Hinglish, a problem that only mentions a product); `first_attempt_problems_max` 3 of 20 (2 of 12 before), `dropped_max` stays 1 | Fake-mode harness passes (no-stack test, `--harness-only`); the real-route numbers for 0.2.0 come from a run on this version, not the installed image |
| 0.2.1 | 26 Nov 2026 | Gates only, the prompt's instructions unchanged (BLD-C205, the cbiz review): the golden set now gates the kind, `kind_accuracy_min` 0.8 over the 6 cases that expect one (5 business ideas), at most one wrong. Before, the kind was reported and not gated, so a model that read every business idea as a problem still passed on its other expectations, and with the assistant on its "problem" outranks the New business area rule | Fake-mode harness passes (kind accuracy 83%, reported on the fake route); enforced on a real route (eval-live, IT's Monday run) |
