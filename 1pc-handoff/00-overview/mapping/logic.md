# 1% Club handoff (draft 0): how the 24 logic modules map onto the back end

Date: Mon 16 Nov 2026. Repo: `kfin-ideas-platform` worktree `.worktrees/handoff` at latest `main`. Pack: `1pc-handoff/` (canvas v106).
Read-only mapping. Nothing in the repo was changed. Paths are relative to the repo root. Everyone the pack names is fictional; this file quotes only placeholders and pack wording.

**Method.** I read every `05-logic/L-*.md` file in full, plus `README.md` §2–5, `00-overview/PRODUCT.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `GAPS.md`, `06-contracts/E-ENTITIES.md` and parts of `A-API.md`. Against those I read the module docstrings and public functions of every `services/worker/*` package, `agents/*/config.yaml` and the prompt heads, `policy/rbac.yaml`, every `apps/web/src/server/*` module, the API route tree, the migration table list, `seed/company-repo/rules/{sla,points}.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` (DEC rows and BLD-082) and the `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` table of contents with §2.

**The short version.** About two-thirds of the worker carries straight over: the gateway, personal-data detection, files, comms, the SLA and job engines, Ones numbers and tiers, impact measurement, voice and embeddings. The pack folds the whole cluster → qualify → score → present → decide → appeal → handover funnel into one review-desk decision, with a 30-day window in which the committee can reverse it. That leaves 7 of the 11 agents, about 10 worker packages and roughly 30 tables with no place in the new design. Three of the 12 non-negotiables collide head-on: **NN4** (blocking at submission against hiding automatically with "Show it"), **NN7** (who sees another person's words, names and files) and **NN8** (checking whether links are reachable). **NN2** bends for "Listen" (text to speech). CLAUDE.md says to stop and ask before any of these change. They need the product lead, with InfoSec or the DPO where noted.
