Questions people ask

Do I need to know how to fix it?

No. Most fixes start with someone describing a problem clearly. If you do have a fix in mind, there's an optional step for it.

What can I share?

Anything that slows down your work, your team, our investors or our distributors. A new business we could build counts too.

What about customer details?

Leave them out. We also check everything you share, files included, and hide personal details such as folio numbers and phone numbers before anyone reads it.

What if it isn't picked?

You still hear why, from the review desk, with the reason in plain words.

Who are the Ones?

Colleagues whose problems became fixes. Their names go up on the Wall and the office screen. It's how we say thank you, not the reason to share.
