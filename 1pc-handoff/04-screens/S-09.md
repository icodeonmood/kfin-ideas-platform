# S-09: A colleague's idea

**Route (proposed):** `/ideas/:id`. **Boards:** `Idea`, `PhoneIdea`

## `S-09.purpose` Purpose

What everyone at KFintech sees of someone else's idea: the short summary, where it is, and one action: Team up.

## `S-09.layout` Layout

1. **Back link** naming where the person came from: "Back to the Wall" / "Back to My ideas" / "Back to IDEA-2026-001481" (tweak `from`).
2. **Head**: status pill, title, reference, "Shared Monday 9 November by Ravi Nair, Investor service, Hyderabad" (`NOT DRAWN:` if the sharer hides their name on the Wall, the name is omitted: "Shared Monday 9 November, Investor service, Hyderabad").
3. **The problem**: the public summary, and the note "This is the short summary everyone at KFintech sees. Ravi's own words stay with him and the review desk."
4. **Where it is**: `C-TRACK` ("Checked and matched: 3 similar ideas linked").
5. **About this idea**: Who it affects; What it's worth; Area; Linked ("3 similar ideas linked"); Who can see it ("Everyone at KFintech, as this short summary").
6. **Team up on this idea** (one card, one journey): "4 colleagues on it. Ravi leads it and decides who joins. The crew helps the review desk with detail and, if it's picked, helps build it." Crew faces. "Send Ravi a note about what you bring. Raised the same thing? Bring your idea along, and if Ravi approves, it joins this one with credit to you." One primary button, "Team up".

## `S-09.states` States (tweak `teamUp`)

| State | The card shows |
|---|---|
| Not asked | "Team up" → `C-SHEET-TEAMUP` |
| Request sent | "Waiting for Ravi" with "Withdraw"; "Ravi reads your note and decides. You'll hear in your Inbox." |
| On the crew | "5 colleagues on it. …" "You're on the crew. You'll get its updates." + "Say hello" (→ `S-08`) |

## `S-09.rules` Rules

1. Nothing private is shown: no raw words, no answers, no files, no reason for a decision beyond the stage.
2. If the idea is closed, withdrawn or merged, Team up is replaced by one line saying so and, for merged, a link to the surviving idea.
3. The sharer opening this URL gets `S-06`; a reviewer gets `S-R2`.
4. Phone: one column; "Team up" sits in the dock.
