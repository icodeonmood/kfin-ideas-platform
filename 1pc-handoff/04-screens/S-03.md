# S-03: Home

**Route (proposed):** `/home`. **Boards:** `Home`, `PhoneHome`

## `S-03.purpose` Purpose

Ask first. The first screen after sign-in asks one question and puts the box to answer it in front of everything else. What the person left unfinished waits below.

## `S-03.hero` Hero (the first screen-height)

1. The question as the heading: returning, "What keeps getting in your way, {me}?"; first visit, "What gets in your way at work, {me}?" (phone: "What's in your way?" / "What gets in your way?").
2. The prompt box (`C-PROMPT`), the brightest thing on the screen, in frosted glass, with the help line and three starters (four on phone, adding "A wait that makes customers give up").
3. Sending from here goes to `S-04B` with the words as the first message; it never opens the empty `S-04` (`P-ROUTES.graph`).
4. While typing, a live sorting guess appears under the box ("Sorting…" then, for example, "Investor service").
5. Attachments can be added here (`C-PROMPT.attach`); the board's `attachments` states show: one file; several files, a folder and a link, with one file too big; the picker open.
6. Under the hero: "Jump back in", a row of four shortcuts with counts that scroll to the sections below: "Your ideas 3", "Find your crew 3" (with three faces), "Share another way 3", and "Needs you" is shown first when it has items. "All my ideas" links to `S-07`.

## `S-03.rules` Rules

1. "Team up" on a crew card opens `C-SHEET-TEAMUP` in place; the card then shows the waiting state.
2. Countdown on the questions card follows the `timeToAnswer` tweak: "3 days left" or, under a day, "07:38 left".
3. The hero stays one calm screen: sections start below the fold on a 900px-tall window.
4. Cinematic arrival; the cards in sections 3 and 5 tilt and morph (`F-MOTION`).
5. Data: `A-API.home` (one call returning needs-you top two with total, ideas top three with total, crew top three with total, ones posts, first-visit flag), plus the sidebar's data.

## `S-03.phone` Phone

A new-chat screen: greeting in open space, the starters as cards above the input, the input pinned above the tab bar. "Jump back in" chips (Drafts 2, Your ideas 3, Find your crew 3, Other ways 3) scroll to the sections, which follow below in the same order. Crew cards and Ones cards scroll sideways with snap. Team up on a phone crew card is an icon button.
