# S-08: An idea's conversation

**Route (proposed):** `/ideas/:id/conversation`. **Boards:** `Messages`, `PhoneMessages`

## `S-08.purpose` Purpose

The one conversation an idea has, opened from the idea or from the Inbox. There is no list of conversations.

## `S-08.layout` Layout

1. **Header**: whose idea ("Your idea" / "The idea"), status pill, reference, a line ("Your idea, shared Saturday 14 November" / "Ravi Nair's idea, Investor service, Hyderabad" / "Your idea, being built by Arjun Rao's team"), and the title (links to the idea's page).
2. **Timeline** (`L-CONVO/3`): system events as single lines; messages as bubbles with avatar, name and role, time; question sets as `C-QCARD`; answers as one message from "Answer" with each answer and "Added to <part>"; files as rows.
3. **Composer**, pinned at the bottom: a line above when questions are open (`L-CONVO/4`); the box with attach; a visibility line ("Only you and the review desk see this conversation." / "The crew and the review desk see this conversation."); "Send" at the right.
4. **Side panel** "In this conversation": participants with what each is doing; and the note "This is the idea's own conversation. It shows on the idea's page too, and new messages reach you in your Inbox, on Teams and by email."

## `S-08.states` States (board tweaks)

| Tweak | States |
|---|---|
| `idea` | Your idea, with the review desk (composer "Message the review desk"); Plain words, with its build team (a build-team message with a spreadsheet "plain-rejection-reasons.xlsx, 12 rows, checked" and a due date "By Thursday 19 November"; composer "Message the build team"); Ravi's idea, after teaming up ("Ravi Nair approved your team-up request. Today, 10:05 am", a welcome from Ravi; composer "Message the crew"); Sanjay's idea, where you were mentioned (the mention highlighted; composer "Reply to Sanjay") |
| `view` | Questions waiting; Answers sent ("Meera's 3 questions, answered", "Answered on Monday 16 November, 4:32 pm. Your answers are below, each added to its part of your idea."); No messages yet ("No messages yet. If the review desk has a question, it shows here and in your Inbox, and we tell you on Teams.") |
| `timeToAnswer` | 3 days left; Under a day ("6 hours left") |

## `S-08.rules` Rules

1. Questions are never answered here; the card's button goes to `S-R3`.
2. Sending with nothing typed or attached: "Write a message or attach a file first." under the box.
3. New messages arrive live (`A-API.idea-events`) and are announced politely; the view stays where the person is reading unless they are at the bottom.
4. Phone: the header collapses to the compact bar (Back, title, status); the composer is pinned above the keyboard; the side panel becomes a sheet behind an "i" button.
