# S-05: Check it, then send it

**Route (proposed):** `/share/:draftId/check`. **Boards:** `Review`, `PhoneReview`

## `S-05.purpose` Purpose

Steps 2 and 3 of 3. The person confirms the draft one part at a time, then sends it. One part is on stage at a time; nothing scrolls on a normal desktop window.

## `S-05.layout` Layout

Under the task bar (steps: "Tell it, done", "Check it" current, "Send it"):

1. **Left rail**: the parts as a numbered list, each with a one-line status. Clicking a part opens it.

| # | Part | Rail status examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your words | "Title, what happens, how often" (+ ", 2 files" if files came from Tell it) |
| 2 | Sorting | "Suggested, not confirmed yet" → "Investor service, saves time, saves cost" |
| 3 | Similar ideas | "2 close ideas found" / "8 similar ideas found" / "Waiting on Ravi to merge" / "Merged into IDEA-2026-001412" / "Ravi kept it separate" |
| 4 | Proof | "Optional" → "2 files" |
| 5 | Your fix | "Optional" → "Added" |
| 6 | Who would help | "Optional" |
| 7 | Send it | "One last look, then submit" |

2. **Stage**: one large glass card to the right of the rail holding the open part: a small counter "Check it, part 1 of 6", a question as the heading, one line of explanation, the content, and a foot row with "Back" (text) at the bottom left and the forward button at the bottom right ("Skip for now" replaces the forward label on an empty optional part). The rail marks the open part as a white card; done parts show a tick, a part needing attention an amber mark.

## `S-05.parts` The parts

1. **Your words.** Heading "Is this how you'd put it?" Line: "This is what the review desk reads first. We wrote it only from your words, so change anything that isn't right." Shows the title, "What happens" (summary), "How often", each with a pencil to change it in place, and the "Hidden from everyone" tile (`C-HIDDEN`) with "Show it". Buttons: "Back to your words" (to `S-04B`) and "Looks right".
2. **Sorting.** Heading "Is it sorted right?" Line: "Sorting decides who reviews it. We suggested these from your words. Confirm them, or change any of them." The four fields of `L-SORT.fields` as chip sets, each with its "Suggested because …" line. The four fields sit as a 2×2 grid in one card, each with its choice rule at the right ("Pick all that apply" / "Pick one") and, where the list is longer, a search field ("Someone else? Search all 6", "Another area? Search all 12"). Forward button "Confirm sorting" confirms. Leaving it unconfirmed and trying to send shows the message under the sorting card: "Confirm these, or change any of them, before you send."
3. **Similar ideas** (`S-05.similar`). Heading "Has someone raised this already?" Line: "Two colleagues shared something close while you were writing. Team up with them, and bring yours along if you like, or keep yours separate." The grid (`C-SIMGRID.grid`): yours first, then the matches, three across. "Preview" opens `C-PREVIEW`; "Team up" opens the sheet with the two-way choice (`L-MERGE.request`). Merge outcomes change this part and add a bar under the steps (`L-MERGE.request/4–7`). `NOT DRAWN:` with no matches the part reads "Nobody has raised this yet." and "Next" skips it.
4. **Proof.** Heading "Anything that shows it?" Line: "Optional, but it helps the review desk weigh it. Add screenshots, files or links that show the problem the way people meet it. We check each one for personal details before anyone else sees it." The uploader and file rows (`C-FILE`), a field "Links to a report, dashboard or ticket", and, when empty, "What helps most": "A screenshot of the SMS or screen", "A call log or ticket count that shows how often", "A report with the time or money it costs". Buttons: "Back", then "Skip for now" (empty) or "Next".
5. **Your fix.** Heading "Got an idea how to solve it?" Line: "Optional. A fix helps the review desk weigh it, but problems on their own get picked too." A row "Say how you'd solve it. A few short questions: how, what it would need and what it's worth. Only the first one is needed." with "Add your fix", which opens the fix sheet (`S-05.fix`). Added: "Included, 5 of 5 ready", the one-line fix, its size line, "Change" and "Remove". Started but not finished: "You started a fix. It isn't included yet." + "Carry on with your fix".
6. **Who would help.** Heading "Who would help?" Line: "Optional. It shows on Find your crew as "They're looking for", so the right colleagues can team up with you." Chips (multi): "Someone at a branch", "Someone in operations", "Someone from tech", "Someone who knows the rules", "Data or reports"; and a field "Who would help, in your own words".
7. **Send it** ("Step 3 of 3"). Heading "Ready to send?" Line: "One last look. You can still change it until the review desk opens it." A summary list, each row with a tick (or an amber mark when it needs attention), its label, its value and "Change" at the right: Title; Sorting (the confirmed line, or "Not confirmed yet"); Similar ideas; Proof ("None added" or the files); Your fix ("None added" or the line); Who would help ("Not said" or the choice). The declaration, always visible beside the button: "By submitting, you confirm there's no customer personal data in it or its files, and it's your own work or you've credited others." Buttons: "Back" and "Submit" (or "Submit with my fix").

## `S-05.fix` The fix sheet

A right-hand sheet (full screen on phone) titled "Your fix": "Optional. Answer what you can; nothing here stops you sending." A readiness line counts what is filled ("2 of 5 ready for the review desk") and names the most useful next answer ("Most useful next: a rough size. A range is fine.").

| # | Question | Input | Help |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How would you solve it?" | Text area. The only required answer | "One or two sentences is plenty. The only part a fix needs." |
| 2 | "What would it need?" (Optional) | Text area | "People, systems, partners or money. Helps size the effort early." |
| 3 | "Has anyone solved this already?" | Single choice: Yes, Partly, No, I don't know | A suggestion may be pre-filled with its basis: "Suggested, your call: distributors on IRIS already see the reason when a transaction is rejected. Investors don't yet." |
| 4 | "What it's worth, roughly" | Any of four measures: Time saved (hours a month), Cost saved (₹ a month), New revenue (₹ a month), Less reliance on outside tools | "Pick any that apply. A rough monthly figure is enough, and a range is fine, written any way: 2 lakh, 2L or 2,00,000." A figure found in the person's words is offered: "From your words: about 70 to 150 hours a month at one branch." + "Use this" |
| 5 | "What is that based on?" | Single choice: My own research, Verified documents, A report or document, A conversation, A hunch | |
| 6 | "What could go wrong, including anything regulatory?" (Optional) | Text area | "Naming risks early builds trust. It never counts against the idea." |

Buttons at the bottom right: "Cancel" and "Add my fix". Sending the sheet without answer 1: "Say how you'd solve it, or close this to send without a fix." After adding, the part shows "Included, 5 of 5 ready", the one-line fix and a size line ("70 to 150 hours a month, about ₹25,000 a month saved, solved elsewhere: partly"). Amounts typed in any Indian form are normalised and read back (`L-WORTH/1`). `OPEN:` this sheet still has a "Cancel" button, unlike the team-up and merge sheets (`P-DECISIONS.open/13`). Full copy: `reference/copy/Review.md`, state `fix=Sheet open`.

## `S-05.states` States (board tweaks)

`step` opens each part. `evidence`: None yet, Added, Upload failed, Personal data found, Link unreachable (`C-FILE.row`). `fix`: Not added, Sheet open, Added. `merge`: None, Waiting for Ravi, Ravi accepted, Ravi declined (`L-MERGE.request`). `similar`: Two close ideas, A full grid of eight. `submit`: Not confirmed yet; Ready; Tried without confirming ("Confirm the sorting first, or change any of it." + "Go to the sorting"); Send failed ("Couldn't send. Your draft is safe. Check your connection, then try again." + "Try again"); Offline ("You're offline. Your draft is saved on this device. Submit now and it sends when you're back online."). `simulatorS2`: Quiet nudge, Check first.

## `S-05.rules` Rules

1. Moving between parts slides forward and back; the rail's current marker moves with it. The parts morph, they do not reload.
2. A part's rail status updates the moment its content changes.
3. Optional parts can be skipped with no extra clicks on the way to Send.
4. Submit conditions and outcomes: `L-SUBMIT.pre`, `L-SUBMIT.send`. On success go to `S-06` "Just shared".
5. Every message sits under its component (`C-FIELD-MSG`). No snackbars.
6. Validation: `V-FIELDS.checkit`.

## `S-05.phone` Phone

The phone board draws Check it as one scrolling page (compact bar with "Step 2 of 3 Check it", three counts at the top: details hidden, sortings, similar ideas; then "Your problem", "Is this sorted right?", similar ideas, proof, fix, and the declaration with "Submit" in the dock). Keep that single-page layout on phone, with the same content, copy and rules as the desktop parts, in the same order; "Who would help" is added after the fix. The dock button is "Submit"; its disabled reason shows above it.
