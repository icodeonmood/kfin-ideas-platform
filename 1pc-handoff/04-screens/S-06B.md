# S-06B: Edit after sharing

**Route (proposed):** `/ideas/:id/edit`. **Boards:** `Edit`, `PhoneEdit`

## `S-06B.purpose` Purpose

Change a shared idea during the edit window. The same content as Check it, on one page, with nothing sent to anyone until Save.

## `S-06B.layout` Layout

1. **Bar**: "Edit IDEA-2026-001481"; "Shared Saturday 14 November. You can edit until the review desk opens it, at the latest Tuesday 17 November."; a save status ("Unsaved changes" → "Saved at 1:39 pm"); "Back to your idea".
2. **Heading**: "Edit your problem" / "Change anything that isn't right. Nothing changes for anyone else until you save."
3. **Your problem** ("The review desk reads this first"): title, What happens, How often, each editable in place; Hidden from everyone with "Show it".
4. **Is this sorted right?** ("Sorting decides who reviews it"): the four chip sets of `L-SORT.fields` with their reasons, and the line "Confirmed when you shared it. You can still change any of these."
5. **"Anything that shows it?"** (Optional): the uploader ("Drop files here, or browse. Screenshots, photos, PDFs or spreadsheets, up to 25 MB each. You can paste a screenshot too."), "Link to a report, dashboard or ticket" with "Add link", file rows with the same failure states as Check it, and "What helps most" when empty.
6. **Your fix** (Optional): "Got an idea how to solve it? It helps the review desk weigh it. Skipping it is fine: problems on their own get picked too." Added (summary, Change, Remove), not added ("Add your fix"), or the sheet open (`S-05.fix`).
7. **Foot**: the declaration, "By saving, you confirm there's no customer personal data in it or its files, and it's your own work or you've credited others."; then, bottom right, "Cancel" (text; discards after an in-place confirm if there are unsaved changes) and "Save changes" (primary).
8. **Side panel** "What happens when you save": "Straight away. The review desk sees the latest version. Earlier versions are kept on the timeline." / "At the latest Tuesday 17 November. Editing closes when the review desk opens it. After that, you can add a note for them instead." / "By Friday 20 November. The review desk decides, and tells you why."

## `S-06B.rules` Rules

1. Rules: `L-SUBMIT.edit`. After Save: "Saved. The review desk will see the latest version." and return to `S-06`.
2. If the desk opens the idea while this screen is open, Save is refused: "The review desk has just opened it, so it can't be changed now. Your changes are kept below. Send them as a message instead." with "Open the conversation" (the unsaved text is carried into the message box). `NOT DRAWN:` default.
3. Sorting stays confirmed unless the person empties a required field; a required field left empty shows "Confirm these, or change any of them, before you send." under the card.
4. Tweaks: `evidence`, `fix`, `save` (Editing / Saved).
5. Phone: same sections in one column; "Save changes" in the dock.
