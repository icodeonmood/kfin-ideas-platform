# S-H4: What happens after you share

**Route (proposed):** `/how-it-works`. **Boards:** `How`

1. `S-H4/1` Title "What happens after you share". The five stages as a vertical track, each with one or two sentences and its promise: Shared ("You get a reference straight away, and your idea is saved as you wrote it." About two minutes); Checked and matched ("Personal details are hidden. Ideas like yours are linked, so you can team up." Automatic, minutes); Reviewed ("The review desk may ask you questions, each with a clear time to answer. You get a decision with a reason." Within 5 working days); Built ("If it's picked, a team builds it. You can join that team." Depends on the fix); Shipped ("We measure what changed. Fixes that make a measured difference make you one of The Ones." After it's live).
2. `S-H4/2` One primary button at the end: "Share your first problem" → `S-04`.
3. `S-H4/3` Shown from Home on a first visit ("What happens next") and linked from Landing's "How it works". The promises come from configuration.
