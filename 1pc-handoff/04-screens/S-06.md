# S-06: Status: your idea's page

**Route (proposed):** `/ideas/:id`. **Boards:** `Status`, `PhoneStatus`

## `S-06.purpose` Purpose

Where a shared idea lives for its sharer: where it is, what happens next and by when, and what the person can do now.

## `S-06.layout` Layout (top to bottom)

1. **Breadcrumb**: "My ideas / IDEA-2026-001481" (the first part names the list state the person came from: "My ideas, Needs you").
2. **The idea card**, full width: a dark card (ink blue shading to a warm brown at the right, radius 28px, white text). Top row: the status pill at the left; at the right "Shared 14-Nov-26", a pencil (Edit) and "Withdraw" while the edit window is open. Then the title (`h1`, white). Under a hairline, one sentence saying where it stands and what happens next, its first clause in white and the rest dimmer.
3. **Where it is**: `C-TRACK`, full width, directly under the card.
4. **The window line**, a blue-wash note hanging from the current stage, with the countdown as a pill at its right: "Open to changes until Tuesday 17 November. Edit or withdraw it any time before the review desk opens it. We tell you on Teams and by email when anything moves." + countdown "3 days left". After opening: "The review desk opened it on Monday 16 November, so the idea itself can't be changed now. Write to them in the conversation."
Below that the page is two columns: the left one (flexible) holds 5 to 7; the right one (about 427px) holds 8 and 9 as two cards.

5. **What you shared** (`h2`): intro "If the review desk puts it on the Wall, everyone at KFintech sees this short summary. Your own words stay with you and the review desk." Then a definition list, label at the left and value at the right, a hairline between rows: The problem, How often, Who it affects, What it's worth, Area, Hidden (chips "Folio ••••89", "Phone ••••10", "+2 more" which opens the list, and "Hidden from everyone" under them). Answers given to the desk show under their part ("From your answer to Meera, Monday 16 November").
6. **Attached** (a row of the same list): file tiles with a type badge, name, "Screenshot, 1.2 MB", "Checked" and "View"; or "Nothing attached. Add one until Tuesday 17 November".
7. **Your fix** (a row of the same list): the fix text clipped to three lines with "Read the full fix"; or "No fix added. Add one until Tuesday 17 November".
8. **You and the review desk** (card): the short view of the conversation. Empty: "No messages yet. If the review desk has a question, it shows up here and in your Inbox, and we tell you on Teams." With questions: `C-QCARD`. Link "Open the conversation" → `S-08`.
9. **Colleagues on something similar** (card): "Matched when you shared. Team up to ask to join their crew, and bring this idea along if you like." Tiles with Preview and Team up (`C-SIMGRID`).

## `S-06.rules` Rules

1. The reference and the next step are readable the instant the page appears; no cover, no wait.
2. Edit → `S-06B`. Withdraw confirms in place on the card with an optional reason field and "Withdraw it"; no pop-up (`L-SUBMIT.withdraw`).
3. When the reviewer revised what it is worth, the tile shows both figures and the reviewer's note (`L-WORTH/3`).
4. A merged idea's page shows where it went: "Merged into IDEA-… by Ravi" or "by the review desk", with the note and a link (`L-MERGE.effects/1`).
5. The page updates live when the desk opens it, asks, or decides (`A-API.idea-events`); a changed region gets one soft blue wash and one polite announcement.
6. Back from here returns to the list state the person came from; after first sharing, Back goes to Home, never to an editable form.
7. Phone: one column in the same order; the track is vertical; the card's actions are in the compact bar's right slot; hidden details open as a bottom sheet.
