# S-04B: Tell it: the conversation (from Home, or a saved draft)

**Route (proposed):** `/share/:draftId`. **Boards:** `ComposeResume`, `PhoneComposeResume`

## `S-04B.purpose` Purpose

The working state of Tell it. Reached three ways: the first message on `S-04`; sending from Home's prompt (the words arrive as the first message and the draft starts building at once); or "Carry on" on a saved draft.

## `S-04B.layout` Layout

Two columns under the task bar (title "Share a problem", steps with "Tell it" current, save status, "Save and close").

**Left: the conversation.**
1. The person's messages, newest last. Each shows their words exactly as given, any attached files as rows (`C-FILE`), who and when ("You, Friday, 8:42 pm, on your phone"), and "Edit".
2. After each message, the assistant's receipt as short lines (`C-DRAFT-PANEL.behaviour/5`), then one status line ("Your draft is ready. Add a detail, or check it." / "Working through your words. You can already add more.") and up to two suggestion buttons.
3. Pinned at the bottom: the prompt box, placeholder "Add a detail, like how often it happens or what you tried", with "Speak instead" and the button "Add to your draft". Help: "Add a detail any time. It goes straight into the draft."
4. When picked up from a saved draft, a line at the top: "Picked up where you left off. Last saved on your phone, Friday 8:42 pm." with "Start a new one instead".

**Right: the draft** (`C-DRAFT-PANEL`), with the loader (`C-DRAFT-LOADER`) at its top whenever parts are being built, and "Move to Review" at the bottom right.

## `S-04B.rules` Rules

1. Editing an earlier message re-runs the draft as if it had been sent that way; pinned parts stay pinned.
2. Adding a detail re-runs only what it could change (`L-DRAFT.parts/4`); the loader plays again for those parts.
3. The steps in the task bar run to the left edge of the draft panel; the task bar spans both columns.
4. Arriving from Home, the hand-over is the flood from the send button; the first message is already in place when content arrives and the loader is already running.
5. Tweaks: `simulatorS1`, `hidden`, `loader` as on `S-04`.
6. Phone: as `S-04.phone`, opening on the conversation with the draft sheet peeking.
