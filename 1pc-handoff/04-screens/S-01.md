# S-01: Landing

**Route (proposed):** `/`. **Boards:** `Landing`, `PhoneLanding`

## `S-01.purpose` Purpose

The signed-out front door. One job: get someone to "Share a problem". Full light, cinematic arrival.

## `S-01.layout` Layout (top to bottom)

1. **Top row**: the brand at the left; two anchor links at the right, "How it works" and "Questions". No sidebar.
2. **Hero**: headline (`d1`, revealed word by word) "Every fix starts with someone who noticed." Lead: "1% Club is where you share the problems you see at work, in your own words. If one is picked, a team fixes it, and you can be part of that team." One primary button, "Share a problem". Under it: "About two minutes. It signs you in with your KFintech account on the way and lands you straight in the box. Been here before? It takes you back in."
3. **Figure** to the right of the hero text: the mark drawn large (it tilts toward the pointer), with a three-line legend under it that names its parts: a ring, "A problem someone noticed"; the slash, "Sharing it: the 1%"; a ring, "The fix a team builds".
4. **How it works**: four numbered steps as a track: "You share it" (You, in about two minutes), "It's reviewed" (The review desk, within 5 working days), "It's built" (A team, if it's picked), "It's shipped" (Everyone). Each with one sentence.
5. **Questions people ask**: the heading at the left, and at the right five questions as an accordion (a plus that turns to a cross), the first open: "Do I need to know how to fix it?", "What can I share?", "What about customer details?", "What if it isn't picked?", "Who are the Ones?"
6. **The Ones so far** (dark slab): "Colleagues who noticed something, said it, and saw it fixed." Three Ones with number, name and their one-line idea.
7. **Closing**: "1% spark. 99% sweat. Bring the spark." and the same primary button.
8. **Footer**: "1% Club, a KFintech programme" and "Part of The Garage".

## `S-01.rules` Rules

1. Both "Share a problem" buttons carry the share intent through sign-in to `S-04` (`L-AUTH.rules/1,4`). A signed-in person who opens `/` goes to `S-03`.
2. The page makes no promise it cannot keep: the times shown come from configuration (`P-PRODUCT.promises`).
3. The Ones shown are the three most recent with public briefs (`A-API.ones`); with none, the slab is omitted.
4. Sections reveal once on scroll (`F-MOTION.arrive/5`). The accordion uses `details`/`summary` semantics and animates its height.
5. Phone: single column; the figure sits under the hero text; the track is vertical; the button is full width. No tab bar (signed out).
