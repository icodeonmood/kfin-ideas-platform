# S-07: My ideas

**Route (proposed):** `/my-ideas`. **Boards:** `MyIdeas`, `PhoneMyIdeas`

## `S-07.purpose` Purpose

Everything the person has shared or teamed up on, where each is, and the one next step for each.

## `S-07.layout` Layout

1. Title "My ideas" and the line "Everything you've shared or teamed up on, where it is and what happens next."
2. The search bar with filters inside (`C-SEARCH`): "Search your ideas", "Filter".
3. A row: two list tabs with counts, "Yours 6" and "Teamed up 3", and at the right the sort control (`C-SORTS`): Latest activity, Date shared, Stage.
4. The list, as a three-column table: **Idea** (title; under it the reference and "Shared Saturday 14 November", or for a draft "Saved yesterday, on this laptop"), **Where it is** (status pill, and under it a five-segment mini track filled up to the current stage; drafts show the pill only), **Next step** (one sentence, then one action at the right end of the row).

## `S-07.rows` Row content by status

| Status | Next-step sentence (example) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Needs you | "The review desk asked you 3 questions on Monday." | "Answer the questions" → `S-R3` |
| Draft | "Two lines so far. Your words are kept." / "About a minute left. Your words and sorting are kept." | "Carry on" → `S-04B` |
| With the review desk | `NOT DRAWN:` in this list; by the pattern of the other rows: "The review desk looks at it by Friday 20 November." | "See where it is" → `S-06` |
| Picked | "A team is being chosen to build it. You'll hear who by Friday 20 November." | "See where it is" |
| Being built | "Being tested at two branches, Hyderabad and Pune." | "See where it is" |
| Shipped | `NOT DRAWN:` what was measured | "See what it changed" |
| Not now | `NOT DRAWN:` the return date | "Read the decision" |
| Not picked / reversed | The reason in one line ("The committee reversed the review desk's not picked: …") | "Read the decision" |
| Withdrawn | `NOT DRAWN:` "Withdrawn on 12 November. It's back in your drafts." | "Restore it" |
| Teamed up (other tab) | "Arjun Rao's idea, you teamed up Tuesday 10 November, IDEA-2026-001412" + its next step | "Open the idea" → `S-09` |

Each action's accessible name includes the idea title ("Answer the questions: Investors call to ask why a redemption was rejected").

## `S-07.filters` Filters

All, Needs you, Drafts, With the review desk, Picked and building, Shipped, Closed (not picked, not now, withdrawn). Each shows a live count for the current tab. They live inside the search bar's panel on desktop and as a sideways chip row on phone.

## `S-07.states` States (board tweaks)

| Tweak | States |
|---|---|
| `filter` | Each filter. A filter with nothing: the filtered empty state, for example "Nothing with the review desk right now. Ideas wait here for up to 5 working days before a decision." with "See 1 in Teamed up" when the other tab has matches, and "Show all ideas". Shipped: "Nothing shipped yet. Ideas land here once they ship and their effect is measured." Closed: "Nothing closed. Ideas that weren't picked or were withdrawn show here, with the reason." |
| `search` | Idle; Suggestions open; Typed: SMS; Asked a question (`C-SEARCH.behaviour`) |
| `list` | Yours; Teamed up |
| `view` | Loaded; Loading (3 skeleton rows); Empty (first time): "Nothing shared yet. Spot something that keeps going wrong? Say it in your own words. It takes about two minutes, and you'll see it here with where it is and what happens next." with "Share your first problem" and the hint "or press P anywhere"; No results |
| `arrival` | Fresh visit; Back from an idea: a quiet line "Back where you were: Yours." and the row that was opened is highlighted once |

## `S-07.return` Coming back

1. Tab, filter, sort, search text and scroll position are restored on Back from any row's destination (`P-ROUTES.state`). They are also in the URL.
2. The restored row is highlighted with one soft wash and receives focus.
3. Changing sort re-orders with gliding rows; changing tab or filter slides the tab underline and fades the list through.

## `S-07.phone` Phone

Title in the top row; the filters as a sideways chip row with counts and ticks; under it a row with the sort cycler ("Sorted by latest activity") and "Copy link"; then the list as cards (title, meta, pill, next step, chevron). Tabs "Yours / Teamed up" sit above the chips. Tab bar at the bottom.

Data: `A-API.my-ideas`.
