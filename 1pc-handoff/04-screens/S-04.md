# S-04: Tell it (a new problem)

**Route (proposed):** `/share`. **Boards:** `Compose`, `PhoneCompose`

## `S-04.purpose` Purpose

Step 1 of 3. The person says the problem in their own words; a draft forms beside it as they go. Nothing here blocks or waits.

## `S-04.empty` Before the first message

1. The screen opens on the same calm prompt as Home, on its own: the question "What keeps getting in your way, {me}?", the prompt box (placeholder "Describe the problem"), the voice hint ("Tap to start speaking", "Not recording until you do"), the help line and the three starters. Focus is in the box.
2. There is no task bar step strip and no draft panel yet. A quiet preview of the draft's slots may show at the right ("Your draft. Forms as you write": "Your title appears here", and "Waiting for your words" under How often, Who it affects, What it's worth, Area) with "Your draft needs a little more first." and a disabled "Move to Review".
3. On the first message, the layout morphs in place into the conversation (`S-04B`): the box moves to the bottom of the left column, the steps appear in the task bar, and the draft panel takes the right column.

## `S-04.states` States (board tweak `state`)

| State | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Empty | `S-04.empty` |
| Typing, draft forming | Text in the box; "Saving…"; the key hint "Enter starts your draft. Shift and Enter for a new line."; parts of the draft "Updating" and filling, each with its why line and "Change" |
| Draft ready | The conversation with the person's message, the receipt, the hidden-details line; the loader running or done; all parts filled; "Move to Review" enabled |
| Speaking | The transcript growing in the box; "Listening. Up to 3 minutes."; the draft updating as words arrive |
| Mic blocked | The warning under the box (`C-PROMPT.voice`) |
| Offline | "Unsaved, you're offline" in the task bar; everything typed is kept; the draft parts already built stay |
| Existing draft prompt | A note above the box: "You have a draft from Friday: … Last saved on your phone, 8:42 pm. Continue it here, or start a new one below." + "Continue that draft" / "Start a new one" |
| Forwarded from email | A banner: "From your email, today at 9:14 am: "Fwd: R07 rejections again". We turned it into this draft. Nothing has been sent." The message is labelled "From your email, 9:14 am" |
| Follow-ups queued | Several added details waiting while the draft rebuilds; "Finishing your last detail first" on the forward button |

Other tweaks: `simulatorS1` (Live draft / Interview, see `P-DECISIONS.experiments`); `hidden` (Several / One: how many details were hidden); `loader` (Plays once / Slow similar ideas / Held for review, see `C-DRAFT-LOADER`).

## `S-04.rules` Rules

1. Everything in `L-DRAFT` and `L-HIDE` applies. The front end never shows an unmasked detail anywhere but the person's own text.
2. "Move to Review" is the one forward action, bottom right of the draft panel. Its helper line explains a disabled state: "Start your draft first. It forms from your words." / "Your draft needs a little more first." / "Still working through your words." / "Finishing your last detail first." / ready: "Looks right? Check the sorting and send it."
3. "Save and close" (task bar) returns to Home; nothing is lost.
4. Leaving by any route (Back, closing the tab, sign-out, session expiry) keeps the draft (`L-DRAFT.autosave`).
5. Field rules: `V-FIELDS.tellit`.

## `S-04.phone` Phone

`NOT DRAWN:` in the current structure. Any language. At least 30 characters, and no fix needed."), the text area with a live count ("30 more characters" → "Enough to work with"), four sentence starters, and the draft as a peek sheet at the bottom ("Your draft. Forms here as you write. Tap to see it."). Build the phone screen with the desktop's conversation model in that layout: the conversation scrolls, the prompt box is pinned above the dock, the draft is the peek sheet with the loader tube under its peek, and the dock holds "Move to Review".
