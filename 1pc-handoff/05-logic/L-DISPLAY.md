# L-DISPLAY: Office screen and launch screen

## `L-DISPLAY.office` Office screen

1. A 1920×1080 wall display, no interaction, no sign-in prompt (served to a display account or by a signed link).
2. Rotates through the Ones, one at a time: tier line, "Number 1", name, flag, their brief with a headline figure, and "1 of 7". Seconds per One is configurable (the board's `secondsPerOne`).
3. The background shows each One as a point of light labelled "No. 1 · Rohit"; the current One's light is brightest. Two background styles exist as an option: "One's own spark" (default) and "Glowing dots".
4. Refreshes its data every few minutes without reloading the page.
5. This screen keeps its own visual style from an earlier design (caps line "1% CLUB · KFINTECH", brighter palette). Do not restyle it to the app's tokens.

## `L-DISPLAY.launch` Launch screen

1. A 1920×1080 screen for the inauguration (Friday 20 November 2026). Three frames: before start (operator instructions, "Start", "Full screen"), the reveal, and the final frame.
2. Pressing Start plays a fixed 30-second sequence with sound: 0:00 black and a bell; 0:04 the spark rises; 0:08 the two zeros arrive; 0:14 to 0:16 the mark completes; 0:16 the tagline "1% spark. 99% sweat."; 0:30 the invitation stays: "You bring the spark. Scan the code to share your idea." with a QR code to the sign-in address.
3. The QR code is generated by the app from the configured sign-in address (`P-DECISIONS.signoff/5`).
4. Sound needs a user gesture; Start is that gesture. If audio fails the visuals still play.
5. One-off use; keep it reachable only to the display operator role.
